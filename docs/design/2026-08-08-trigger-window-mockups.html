WHY IT WARNS
A boss healing itself undoes the whole pull. This is the one group that is worth a warning even when you can do nothing else — someone has to interrupt it, and the log names the spell before it lands.
WHAT IT CATCHES
Lord Nagafen begins to cast Complete Heal.
a froglok shin knight begins to cast Greater Healing.
Cazic Thule begins to cast Gate.
Curated, not a pattern
This one is decided by a spell table that is checked against real sessions, not by a regex you could edit. Switching it off is the whole control it offers.
Want one it misses? Write your own trigger.

The ALERTS section (warn-about groups, presets, sound, summons, crowd control) and the BOSS TIMERS section are gone from here. They were always the same question a trigger pack answers — “what puts something on my screen” — and answering it in two places meant a pack could be on while the surface it draws to was off, with nothing saying so.
