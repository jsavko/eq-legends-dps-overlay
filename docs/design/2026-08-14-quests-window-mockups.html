Plane of Sky Quests

A fourth reading surface (tray → Quests…), on the History/Triggers/Session model: three fixed panes, warm parchment palette, every click swaps content inside a pane and nothing reflows. Rail = all 95 class-test quests grouped by class. Middle = the selected quest: reward, stats, quest NPC, and the turned-in toggle. Right = the item checklist. All data below is Rhale's real ledger (live log, backfilled) joined with the real eqlposky.com export of Aug 14.

A · the window

Two kinds of truth, never conflated. The looted count (right column) is a fact from the log and nothing can edit it. The owned checkbox and the quest's turned-in toggle are claims — set by hand or by an eqlposky import, both editable, neither ever inferred from the counts beside them. A done quest dims in the rail with a teal ✓; class headers count them. The player's own class is not something the app can know, so the rail simply remembers which class was last selected and pins nothing.

B · why the panes split this way

The rail answers "where am I across all sixteen classes" — every quest visible, done ones dimmed with a ✓, in-progress ones showing owned-of-needed. The middle answers "what is this quest and is it finished" — the toggle lives beside the reward it claims, not in the checklist, because "turned in" is a fact about the QUEST while the checkboxes are facts about ITEMS. The right pane answers "what do I still need and who drops it", sources verbatim from the dataset. Selecting anything swaps content on the same pixels; the window never reflows — the History window's contract, kept.

Not in v1, on purpose: item icons (would hotlink a fan site's CDN), a per-class achievement readout (the game's /ach already answers it), and auto-detecting turn-ins from the log's "You offered 1 Crude Wooden Flute to Cilin Spellsinger." lines — that wording turned out to exist after all, but folding it in deserves its own pass with its own rules; the toggle stays manual and honest until then.
