2 · The info dialog, rebuilt

Four sections replacing the “Curated, not a pattern” callout. Every pattern shown is read live from rules.js and spellwatch.js — none of it is written out by hand, so it cannot drift from what the parser is actually running. The last section is the honest half: a copy of the pattern is a starting point, not an equal.

BUILT-IN RULE

Heals & gates

interrupt it or the kill resets

WHY IT WARNS

A boss healing itself undoes the whole pull. This is the group worth a warning even when there is nothing else you can do about it — someone has to interrupt, and the log names the spell before it lands.

HOW IT MATCHES

The log line it reads — rules.js · cast-start

  • ^(.+?) beg(?:ins|in) casting (.+?)\.$

…and which of those casts belong here — spellwatch.js · 2 patterns

  • heal  \bheal(?:ing|s)?\b|\bchloroplast\b|\brenew\w*\b|\bregrowth\b|\b(?:celestial|sacred|renewing) echo\b
  • gate  \b(?:gate|succor|evacuate)\b

WHAT IT CATCHES

  • A ghoul savant begins casting Superior Healing.
  • Emalina begins casting Greater Healing V.
  • Steven begins casting Gate.

WRITTEN AS A TRIGGER

^(?<mob>.+?) beg(?:ins|in) casting (?<spell>(?=[^.]*(?:\b[hH]eal(?:[iI]ng|[sS])?\b|\b[cC]hloroplast\b|\b[rR]enew\w*\b|\b[rR]egrowth\b|\b(?:[cC]elestial|[sS]acred|[rR]enewing) [eE]cho\b|\b(?:[gG]ate|[sS]uccor|[eE]vacuate)\b)).+?)\.$

SHOW

${mob} — ${spell}

What the copy will not know

The pattern above is the real one, and it is still only half of what this rule does. A copy fires on any line that matches — including one a player typed into /general.

  • Whether the caster is an enemy. The built-in rule checks the roster and what the fight has engaged first.
  • How loud to be. It ranks a heal above a root; a copy draws every match the same way.
  • That an interrupt cancels it — the built-in warning clears itself when the log confirms one.
  • Your six SHOW switches. A copy fires whatever preset you are on.
