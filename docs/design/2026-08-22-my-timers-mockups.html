My timers — panel mockups

A second countdown panel for the player’s own timers, placed independently of the boss panel. Jade rather than ember so the two are told apart by colour at a glance. Rows are fixed-height in every state and never re-sorted — the boss panel’s rules, held here for the same reason. Three variants of one row; everything else is identical.

Variant A — the bar is the row

The boss panel’s trick at a larger size: the bar is the row’s background, draining right-to-left underneath the text. Smallest footprint, and the two panels read as siblings. 44px rows.
