    Who's tracked

    Which players get a row on the meter. Leave it empty and everyone the log sees shows up — that is the default and what you want in a group. In a public zone, pick the people you care about and the rest disappear from the meter.

        This hides rows and nothing else. Every number on the people you keep is identical either way — clear the list and it is the same fight with more rows in it. The list belongs to this character alone.

        Pets

        A summoned pet gets a generated name — "Kibektik", "Goneker" — and the log never says whose it is. Unmapped, it gets its own row; mapped, its damage folds into its owner. Pets written Rhale`s warder need no entry at all.

            Mappings in force

              You can also map one without leaving the game: type pet Kibektik = Khanvikt into any chat channel.

              Getting the OWNER wrong is the half that hurts. A mistyped pet name simply matches nothing, but a mistyped owner folds real damage into somebody who does not exist and grows a phantom row beside the real one. That is why the owner is a name you pick.

              Hotkeys

              Global, so they work while EverQuest has focus.

              Electron accelerator syntax, e.g. Control+Shift+L. A binding another program already owns will be reported in the overlay.

              Triggers

              Everything that can warn you — the rules this app ships with, GINA packages you import, and triggers you write yourself — lives in one window, with a switch for each. Control+Shift+A still mutes the lot for a pull without losing any of those choices.

              Session stats

              The other half of a play night: what you killed, looted, earned, and advanced. Off by default — nothing below runs, or costs anything, until you switch it on.

              Record

              Each of these is checked before its patterns run, so anything switched off costs nothing at all rather than being recorded and then hidden.
